Motion and physics

Motion starts on. Choosing a layout gathers the stars through the centre, then lets them squirm into the new form. The default Constellation is one continuous field—relationships stay available in links and selection, rather than becoming visual blobs. Centre pull, repel, relation tether, link tension and link distance act in the all-star visual field; move the pointer through the graph to deflect nearby stars. Solid collision physics runs for a direct drag and release.
